No picture is stored or copied. The only recorded sounds are the CC0 ones in `public/sounds/`; the rest is made in code. The aurora scene began from one photograph its author showed as a mood: a small figure with a light under a snowy peak and a green aurora. Nothing of that photograph is in the repo, and its place was not reproduced: the ridge, the horns, the fjord, the cabin and the sky here are this demo's own, made in code.
